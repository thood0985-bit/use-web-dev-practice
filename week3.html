What are the best songs?

One of my favorite songs is Dear Theodosia. I think the songs are ranked in the following order:

Say No to This

My number one Hamilton song and one of my favorite songs from the musical.

Dear Theodosia

Aaron Burr and Alexander Hamilton sing about their children and their hopes for the future.

The Schuyler Sisters

This song introduces the Schuyler sisters and is one of the most memorable songs from Hamilton.
